Executive Summary

Core view: Yasho delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with revenue up to ₹307.74 crore, EBITDA margin at 24.2% and PAT at ₹36.05 crore, while capex guidance was stepped up to ₹250 crore. The setup supports growth, but the investment case now depends on timely capacity commissioning and cash conversion.

Yasho operates in specialty and performance chemicals with a meaningful export mix and customer-led custom manufacturing exposure. Management commentary points to sustained demand and a multi-year growth runway, but this must be reconciled with execution on new assets and disciplined working capital.

Business Position

Strengths

  • Specialty-chemicals platform with export relationships and repeat industrial demand.
  • Q1 volume growth indicates broad-based customer off-take.
  • Improving working-capital cycle versus the prior year.
  • Guided margin stability around current run-rate levels.

Constraints

  • Growth assumptions depend on large capex execution over FY27-FY28.
  • Raw-material spreads and export demand cycles remain volatile.
  • Leverage and working capital can rise during expansion phases.
  • At elevated multiples, any commissioning delay can de-rate the stock.

Valuation

At ₹4,187.50 (11 September 2026), market capitalization is approximately ₹7,161 crore. The stock is discounting a successful multi-year ramp with stable margins and healthy conversion of capex into earnings.

The current valuation can be supported only if growth and utilization milestones hold; any miss on commissioning, working capital or margin discipline can compress multiples quickly.

Key Risks

  • Capex commissioning delays or lower-than-planned utilization.
  • Raw-material and energy volatility affecting specialty-chemicals margins.
  • Export-demand slowdown in key geographies.
  • Working-capital expansion diluting operating cash flow.
  • Valuation de-rating if growth cadence falls below assumptions.
