Summary

Core view: Dee Development Engineers grew Q1 FY27 revenue 31.6% year-on-year and PAT 22.4%, but both profit and EBITDA margin fell materially quarter-on-quarter due to cost pressure and deferred revenue dispatches. The heavy capex cycle appears to be ending, which should help asset utilisation and cash flow, but near-term profitability normalization is not yet complete, and the stock trades at a rich ~60x trailing P/E.

Dee Development Engineers manufactures process piping systems (spools, fittings, prefabricated pipe systems) for oil & gas, power and process industries, with a large recent capex cycle aimed at capacity expansion.
