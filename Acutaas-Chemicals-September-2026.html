Summary

Core view: Acutaas Chemicals grew Q1 FY27 revenue 48.5% and PAT 70.4% year-on-year, but both fell sharply quarter-on-quarter (revenue -25.3%, PAT -44.2%) from an exceptional Q4 FY26. At about 74x trailing earnings, the valuation already assumes continued strong growth, so the sequential decline is a meaningful signal to watch rather than a footnote.

Acutaas Chemicals (formerly Ami Organics) manufactures specialty and pharma intermediates and APIs, serving both regulated pharma and broader specialty-chemicals customers.

Business Quality And Mix

Strengths

  • Strong YoY growth in both revenue (+48.5%) and PAT (+70.4%).
  • High ~34% operating margin reflects specialty/pharma-intermediate positioning.
  • Diversified across pharma and specialty-chemicals end markets.
  • History as a well-regarded specialty-chemicals manufacturer (formerly Ami Organics).

Constraints

  • Sharp sequential decline (-25.3% revenue, -44.2% PAT) from an exceptional Q4 FY26.
  • Trailing P/E of about 74x is rich and prices in continued strong growth.
  • Specialty/pharma-intermediate demand can be lumpy, tied to customer order cycles.
  • 52-week range of ₹1,290-₹3,735 shows high stock volatility.
