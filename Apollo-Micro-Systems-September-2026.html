Apollo Micro Systems

A fast-scaling strategic electronics and munitions platform whose acquisition-led growth, capacity ambition and order visibility must now translate into margins and operating cash.

Executive Summary

Core view: Reported Q1 growth was acquisition-led: consolidated revenue rose 88.1%, while standalone organic growth was 16.7%. The ₹1,704 crore order book and Unit-3 expansion support growth, but margin dilution, negative cash flow, debt and promoter pledge keep execution risk high.

Apollo develops electronic and electromechanical subsystems for missiles, naval warfare, avionics, space and homeland security. Ideal Explosives extends the group into explosives, munitions and warheads, creating strategic breadth but breaking year-on-year comparability.

Business Position

Strengths

  • Qualified presence in missile, naval, avionics and space programmes.
  • Ideal adds explosives and warhead capabilities.
  • Consolidated order book of ₹1,704 crore on 8 August 2026.
  • Unit-3 can materially expand addressable production.

Constraints

  • Q1 growth is not like-for-like after acquisition.
  • FY26 CFO was negative ₹130 crore.
  • Borrowings reached ₹543 crore.
  • About 30.5% of promoter holding was pledged.

Valuation

At ₹422.25 on 11 September 2026, market capitalization was approximately ₹15,691 crore and trailing P/E about 129.3x. The price already discounts rapid order conversion, acquisition integration and a successful Unit-3 ramp.

Key Risks

  • Acquisition-led figures obscure organic performance.
  • Unit-3 commissioning or customer approvals slip.
  • Negative CFO and working-capital needs force more financing.
  • Debt and promoter pledge increase financial risk.
  • Domestic procurement remains lumpy; Q1 exports were nil.
