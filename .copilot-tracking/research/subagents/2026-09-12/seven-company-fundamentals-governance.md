## Findings

### Financial calculation method

Where annual and comparable quarterly scopes align, the bridge is:

TTM = latest annual + latest reported period - prior-year comparable period.

Direct Screener TTM values are external cross-checks. Small differences are
retained when local inputs are rounded or source snapshots differ. Foseco's
acquisition consolidation and changed share count make the direct TTM figure
safer than a synthetic EPS bridge.

### Technical chart labels

All seven stored polylines contain 52 points, not 51. The renderer stores only
SVG coordinates and has no date array. Exact month labels therefore require
the sampled trading dates from the data-generation source.

If actual dates cannot be recovered, treat the points as uniformly sampled
observations over an approximate interval from 11 September 2025 through
11 September 2026. For point i among N points, map:

t(i) = t0 + i / (N - 1) * (t1 - t0).

Use sparse anchors such as Sep '25, Dec '25, Mar '26, Jun '26, and Sep '26.
Mark the axis as approximate. Anchor the final observation to 11 September
2026 or the nearest available trading session. Do not fabricate weekends or
holidays and do not claim consecutive weekly sampling without the original
resampling method.
