investor presentations, earnings-call transcripts published by the company, and

the required dated market observation is unavailable from a primary source. Facts

### Business, customers, and competitive position

J.G. is India's largest zinc oxide manufacturer and zinc recycler, with more
than 90 customized grades. It serves more than 200 domestic and 50 global
customers in more than ten countries. More than 95% of sales are direct to end
customers, repeat customers exceed 90%, and the customer set includes nine of
the ten largest global tyre makers and all major Indian tyre makers. The
company does not disclose customer-level revenue concentration.

FY26 revenue mix was about 85.0% rubber and tyre, 8.2% pharma and chemicals,
3.7% agriculture, and 3.1% other applications. The comparable FY24 rubber and
tyre share was 89.7%. Management targets non-rubber revenue rising from about
15% to 30% over three to four years. Domestic and export revenue percentages
were not stated in the extracted presentation; the company identifies more
than ten export countries and says its India exports are concentrated in
Australia, other SAARC markets, Thailand, and Europe.

Current installed capacity is approximately 70,000 MTPA of zinc chemicals,
plus recycled-zinc-ingot capacity. Plant detail is 14,400 MTPA zinc oxide and
5,040 MTPA ingot at Jangalpur; 1,800 MTPA zinc oxide at Belur; and 43,704 MTPA
zinc oxide, 2,016 MTPA ingot, and 10,080 MTPA zinc sulphate and allied chemicals
at Naidupeta. Naidupeta has IATF, WHO GMP, and pharmacopoeia credentials.

### Guidance, catalysts, and risks

Dahej is a 40,000-plus MTPA, INR 100 crore internally funded project with
management-estimated revenue potential of INR 900 crore at full utilization.
Phase 1, 15,000-17,000 MTPA of zinc oxide, is targeted for November 2026.
Management expects FY28 utilization of at least 50%-60%, moving toward the 70s
in FY29 before Phase 2. Phase 1 revenue potential is INR 300-400 crore at an
11%-12% EBITDA margin. The wider long-term consolidated margin aspiration is
14%-15%, supported by pharma, specialty chemical, cosmetics, and electronics
mix. These are management aspirations, not established run-rate economics.

Other catalysts are about 5,000 tonnes of Naidupeta debottlenecking, western
India freight savings, ceramics customer seeding, new LabPure and JG-ZRA grades,
zinc sulphate growth, and the still-unquantified JG TUR recycled-rubber project.
Risks include 85% tyre exposure, zinc-dross sourcing and energy costs, working
capital, execution and ramp-up at Dahej, customer approval timing, commodity
pass-through lags, and the risk that premium-product mix develops more slowly
than management expects.

323.25 crore. Reported EBITDA was INR 18.46 crore at 5.47%; after a INR 5.58
crore one-time settlement expense, net EBITDA was INR 12.88 crore at 3.82%.
PBT loss was INR 40.73 crore and PAT loss INR 30.86 crore. The order book was
about INR 274 crore.

The secondary consolidated history illustrates why normalization is essential:

| INR crore | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 |
|-----------|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|
| Revenue | 66 | 44 | 67 | 315 | 323 |
| PAT | -431 | -91 | 254 | 3 | -31 |

FY24 PAT contains exceptional/non-operating effects and is not evidence of a
sustainable earnings turnaround. Historical EPS is also distorted by the rights
issue and is not used in valuation.

Q1 FY27 standalone revenue was INR 78.6 crore, up 25%; EBITDA INR 3.4 crore, up
223%, at 4.3%; and PAT loss INR 7.2 crore. Consolidated revenue was INR 117.1
crore, EBITDA INR 7.32 crore at 6.2%, and net worth INR 272 crore. Consolidated
PAT remained a INR 4.75 crore loss. Tulip contributed only from May 2026 and
reported INR 39 crore revenue and INR 3.96 crore EBITDA in the included period,
so the year-on-year consolidated comparison is not organic.

Standalone order book at Q1 was INR 211 crore, including INR 187 crore products
and INR 24 crore aftermarket. Consolidated order book was INR 539 crore,
including INR 406 crore products and INR 133 crore aftermarket; enquiry pipeline
was INR 490 crore. Material orders include INR 66.75 crore for 25 loaded
ReachStackers from CONCOR, about INR 110 crore for roughly 170 military cranes,
and a CONCOR O&M contract above INR 30 crore.

Products include cranes, ReachStackers, forklifts, container handlers, and
defence systems. Disclosed customers include Bharat Dynamics, BEL, BEML, Indian
Army, Indian Air Force, ONGC, Coal India, Reliance, Tata Steel, Adani, and NTPC.

### Balance sheet, guidance, and risks

The secondary FY26 consolidated cross-check shows borrowings around INR 284
crore and deeply negative operating cash flow near INR 131 crore. Q1 consolidated
borrowings were around INR 382 crore. These figures require scope reconciliation
against rights proceeds and Tulip consolidation before enterprise valuation.

Management sees Tulip growing 20%-40% annually based on recent history and
achieving medium-term EBITDA margins of 14%-15%. TIL standalone aspires to
15%-16%, but management explicitly described the horizon as three to seven years
and did not provide precise current-year guidance. TIL may increase its Tulip
stake from 60% toward 74%; the board approved up to INR 50 crore of further
equity infusion.

Catalysts are order conversion, defence acceptance, aftermarket growth, Tulip
full-quarter consolidation, rights-funded liquidity, and operating leverage.
Risks are ongoing PAT losses, high finance cost, working capital, rights/call-
money dilution, imported components and currency, defence delivery timing,
Tulip integration and minority leakage, product-mix volatility, and Chinese
price and credit competition.

### Turnaround valuation

Until recurring PAT and cash flow turn positive, use normalized EV/revenue or
EV/EBITDA, not P/E. Current market capitalization was about INR 2,100 crore and
the secondary debt estimate about INR 382 crore, implying an enterprise value
near INR 2,480 crore before cash adjustment. This is more than 5x the Q1 revenue
run rate and already discounts a large turnaround.

Illustrative EV/revenue framework:

| Year | Bear | Base | Bull |
|------|------|------|------|
| FY27E | INR 500 crore revenue at 2.0x EV/revenue | INR 600 crore at 2.5x | INR 700 crore at 3.0x |
| FY28E | INR 650 crore at 1.8x | INR 850 crore at 2.3x | INR 1,050 crore at 2.8x |
| FY29E | INR 800 crore at 1.6x | INR 1,100 crore at 2.1x | INR 1,400 crore at 2.6x |

Equity value equals enterprise value less net debt and minority interests. Do
not convert these cases into per-share values until debt, cash, Tulip minority,
and fully paid rights shares are reconciled.
