## Cross-company valuation framework

The model choices below are methodological recommendations, not valuation
outputs. Multiples and forecast inputs must be populated only after the missing
financial evidence is extracted.

* **Varroc Engineering:** primary EV/EBITDA because leverage and international
	manufacturing assets make capital structure material; secondary P/E as an
	equity-holder cross-check after normalising exceptional items.
* **Electronics Mart India:** primary P/E because retail earnings, inventory
	turns, and store productivity drive equity value; secondary lease-adjusted
	EV/EBITDA to compare operators with different owned-versus-leased footprints.
* **Sundram Fasteners:** primary P/E for a mature, profitable auto-component
	franchise; secondary EV/EBITDA to control for capex cycles and leverage.
* **Happy Forgings:** primary EV/EBITDA because forging capacity additions are
	capital intensive; secondary P/E after new capacity reaches steady state.

	normalise.

	utilisation, and debt are central to economics; secondary adjusted net asset
	value using independently supportable fleet values, with P/E only as a
	tertiary cycle check.
