Executive Summary

Core view: Q1 growth remained healthy, with revenue up 15.9% and adjusted PAT up about 25%. Stable gross margin near 39% supports the value-addition thesis, though adjusted EBITDA margin softened slightly year on year.

Pitti supplies electrical laminations, motor and generator cores, die-cast rotors and machined components. Its integrated process chain and customer qualifications are competitive strengths, while acquisitions broaden capability and raise integration complexity.

Business Position

Strengths

  • Integrated laminations-to-machining capability.
  • Long qualification cycles support customer stickiness.
  • Exposure to rail, power, industrial motors and renewables.
  • FY23-FY26 total-income CAGR near 20%.

Constraints

  • Heavy machinery and working-capital requirements.
  • Acquisition and capacity-ramp execution risk.
  • Customer programs can be lumpy.
  • Adjusted metrics exclude ESOP cost.

The durable growth metric is value added per tonne. Revenue growth driven only by steel throughput would produce lower returns than a rising share of machined and assembled components.

Valuation

At ₹1,168 on 11 September 2026, market capitalization was approximately ₹4,398 crore and the trailing P/E was about 35.3x. The multiple prices in sustained growth and successful capacity monetization, while leverage means enterprise value is materially higher than equity value.

Key Risks

  • Capacity additions ramp more slowly than planned.
  • Steel-price pass-through and customer concentration.
  • Acquisition integration and leverage.
  • Working-capital absorption weakens free cash flow.
  • Difference between adjusted and reported profit widens.
