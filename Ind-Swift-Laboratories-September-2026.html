Summary

Core view: Ind-Swift Laboratories has completed a major restructuring, selling its API and CRAMS business and becoming virtually debt-free as of July 2025. The company now focuses mainly on formulations, with trailing revenue of about ₹665 crore and net profit of about ₹57.3 crore, and a return on equity of only about 4-6%, reflecting an earnings base still being rebuilt after the restructuring.

Ind-Swift Laboratories previously operated API, CRAMS and formulations businesses; following the divestment of API/CRAMS, it is now primarily a formulations company with a much-improved balance sheet but still-modest returns on capital.

Business Quality And Mix

Strengths

  • Virtually debt-free following the July 2025 restructuring.
  • Focus on formulations reduces exposure to API price cycles.
  • Stock has re-rated sharply (1-year return of roughly 240-255%) as the restructuring de-risked the balance sheet.
  • Simplified business model post-divestment.

Constraints

  • Return on equity of only about 4-6% is modest for a formulations business.
  • Earnings base is still being rebuilt after the API/CRAMS divestment, making historical comparisons less useful.
  • No dividend paid in recent years.
  • Trailing P/E of about 57-58x is rich for the current return profile.
