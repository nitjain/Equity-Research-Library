Summary

Core view: Varroc Engineering posted its highest-ever quarterly revenue of ₹2,634 crore, up 29.9% year-on-year, with EV-model revenue up 87% and now about 16% of the total. However, PAT fell 27-28% as EBITDA margin compressed to 8.4-8.5% from 9.5-9.6%, and the auditor flagged a qualified opinion related to the ongoing TYC arbitration matter, which investors should not overlook despite the strong top line.

Varroc Engineering supplies lighting, electronics and precision plastic/metallic components to two-wheeler, three-wheeler, passenger-vehicle and commercial-vehicle OEMs, with a growing EV-model revenue mix, since divesting its four-wheeler lighting business.

Business Quality And Mix

Strengths

  • Record quarterly revenue since the four-wheeler lighting divestment.
  • EV-model revenue grew 87% YoY, now about 16% of total revenue.
  • Strong growth across all vehicle segments (2W, 3W, PV, CV).
  • Overseas revenue grew 45.6% YoY, faster than the 28.6% India growth.

Constraints

  • EBITDA margin compressed to 8.4-8.5% from 9.5-9.6% YoY.
  • PAT fell 27-28% YoY despite record revenue, showing real cost/margin pressure.
  • Auditors flagged a qualified opinion relating to the ongoing TYC arbitration matter.
  • Margin compression alongside legal-risk overhang both need resolution before the stock can be seen as fully de-risked.
